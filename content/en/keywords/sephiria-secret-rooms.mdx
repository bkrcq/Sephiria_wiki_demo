## Direct answer

Hidden rooms in Sephiria are **real and officially documented** — TEAM HORAY's patch notes reference them directly, including what they can contain. What does not exist is an official map of every hidden room. This page gives you the documented locations, entry methods, and contents from the patch notes, with the update number attached to each fact, so you know exactly what is confirmed and what is still player folklore.

*Checked against TEAM HORAY's official Steam patch notes on October 21, 2026.*

## Documented hidden-room facts

| Fact | Update |
| --- | --- |
| Hidden rooms exist as a stage feature; their rewards were changed — "the hidden room rewards in each stage have varied" | 0.10.10 (Jan 22, 2026) |
| The **Mystic Pot** can appear in hidden rooms (it also appears as a combat reward); inserting artifacts into it creates a Tablet | 0.10.10 (Jan 22, 2026) |
| **Rooted Cave** (an early stage) contains a hidden room; a bug where players could not escape it was fixed | 0.10.10 (Jan 22, 2026) |
| The **Library** has a secret room entered **through cracks**; NPC dialogue volume inside it was adjusted | 0.10.14 (Feb 16, 2026) |
| Hidden rooms appear in **some events in Chapter 4's Woodlands stage** — specifically in events labeled as not granting achievements | 0.10.0 (Dec 24, 2025) |
| A **Giant Mushroom** object is tied to hidden-room rewards: when its HP reaches 0 it grants 30% of the achieved reward count | 0.10.15 (Feb 18, 2026) |

## What the record supports, practically

- **Look for cracks in the Library.** It is the only entry method stated in official notes: the Library's secret room is reached through cracks.
- **Hidden rooms are reward spaces, not just shortcuts.** Varied per-stage rewards, the Mystic Pot, and the Giant Mushroom mechanic all point the same way: find a hidden room and expect loot or a conversion service, not a boss.
- **They are event- and stage-dependent.** The Chapter 4 notes attach hidden rooms to specific events, and the 0.10.10 notes vary rewards by stage — a hidden room is not guaranteed in every run or every stage.
- **The system is newer than the game.** Hidden rooms first appear in the documented record in December 2025 (0.10.0), so Early Access-era guides from before then cannot describe them.

## What is still not documented

No official source lists every hidden room, its exact trigger tile, or a per-room reward table. Claims of a complete secret-room map should be treated as unverified unless they carry chapter, room, version, and repeatable evidence. If you document a room, record: chapter and stage, how you entered, what was inside, game version, and a screenshot — the standard used across this wiki.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there secret rooms in Sephiria?

Yes. Official patch notes document hidden rooms in multiple stages, including Rooted Cave, the Library (entered through cracks), and Chapter 4's Woodlands events.

### What is inside Sephiria's secret rooms?

Documented contents and mechanics include the Mystic Pot (convert artifacts into a Tablet), varied stage-specific rewards, and a Giant Mushroom object that grants 30% of the achieved reward count at 0 HP.

### Is there a complete Sephiria secret room map?

No official map exists, and no complete community map with reproducible evidence was identified in the sources checked on October 21, 2026.

### When were hidden rooms added to Sephiria?

They first appear in the official patch-note record in update 0.10.0 (December 24, 2025), with reward changes in 0.10.10 (January 2026).
