## Release and purchase evidence sheet

Use this sheet when an old review, an old price, and a newer patch are being quoted together. It separates three clocks: when a game change shipped, when a base price changed, and when reviews were counted. It does not predict a discount, a future patch, or a console edition.

**Evidence cutoff: October 21, 2026.** This page reorganizes the repository's existing source record; it reports no new verification. The registry date uses that evidence cutoff for this retrospective compilation, not a claim that this page was published or newly checked then.

## Review arithmetic and window check

The October 21 official summary records 12,746 positive and 739 negative reviews: together **13,485**. Dividing positive reviews by the total gives about **95%** after rounding. Keep the **12,537 recommendations** figure under its own label; substituting it for the review total changes the calculation.

An earlier **93% recent-review** research snapshot uses a different review window. Comparing that directly with an all-time share cannot establish improving reception. If you collect another snapshot yourself, keep both its date and its filter or window.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is $17.99 a guaranteed current price?

No. It is the base price in the recorded September 14, 2026 change. Use your own storefront for a purchase quote.

### Does 95% prove reviews improved from 93%?

No. The recorded all-time and recent-review windows differ.

### Does 1.0.33 mean there have been no later patches?

No. It is the limit of the verified patch record used here, not a fresh search for later updates.

### Does the timeline establish a Switch release?

No. Steam milestones and prices do not establish a Nintendo product. Use the [platform evidence worksheet](/guides/sephiria-switch) for platform claims.
