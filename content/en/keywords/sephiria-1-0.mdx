## What 1.0 is

Sephiria **version 1.0 launched on July 31, 2026**, ending an Early Access period that TEAM HORAY's launch announcement measures at **484 days and 58 updates** (the project itself began at the end of 2021, eight years after the studio's previous game, Dungreed). Everything in this page comes from that announcement and the official Steam patch notes, checked October 21, 2026.

## What the 1.0 launch added

From the official launch announcement, the full-release content totals are:

- **6 chapters** — Chapter 6 is new in 1.0, with new cutscenes and quests, **2 new bosses and 1 new mini-boss**, 3 new BGM tracks, and 3 new achievements.
- **The ending** — a full ending sequence with cutscene and credits; mid-run saves from Early Access are not carried into the new chapter.
- **Six weapon branches with over 200 upgrades** — 1.0 itself added 1 new Great Sword upgrade and 4 new Dagger upgrades.
- **About 300 Artifacts, about 70 Tablets, over 30 Potions.**
- **Over 20 costumes.**
- **18 Hard Mode elements.**
- **12 languages at launch** (15 are listed on the store page now) and **online co-op for up to 4 players**.
- **A tribute badge** shown in multiplayer for everyone who purchased the game before the official release.

The launch post also bundles the **1.0.19 content additions**: 3 new Miracles (**Angler**, **Electromancer**, **Flame Sword**), 19 new Artifacts, and 7 new Tablets.

## Frequently asked questions

### When did Sephiria 1.0 release?

July 31, 2026, on Steam for Windows and macOS.

### What is the latest Sephiria version?

1.0.33, released September 11, 2026, per the official Steam news feed checked on October 21, 2026.

### Did 1.0 add the ending?

Yes. The launch notes state a game ending — including its cutscene and credits — was added with Chapter 6 in version 1.0.

### Is Sephiria still being updated after 1.0?

Yes. Nine documented updates landed between launch day and September 11, 2026 (see the table above).
