## Direct answer

**There is no official Sephiria tier list**, and this page will not fabricate one. TEAM HORAY has never published a ranking, and the patch cadence — balance changes in nearly every update from 0.7.x through 1.0.33 — means any unsourced S/A/B list is obsolete on arrival. What exists, and what this page gives you, is the documented material a real tier list would have to be built from: six branches, their unlocks, their roles, and their dated balance history.

*Verified against TEAM HORAY's Steam patch notes on October 21, 2026.*

Heavy patch attention cuts both ways — it marks the branches the developer watches most closely, not the strongest ones.

## What a valid tier list would require

1. **A game version.** Balance moved in 1.0.24, 1.0.30, 1.0.31, and 1.0.33 within six weeks of launch; a ranking without a version stamp is fiction.
2. **A test protocol.** Same chapter, same mode, recorded runs — the [Weapons page](/weapons) publishes one you can reuse.
3. **Separation by role.** A guard branch, a ranged branch, and a state-switching branch solve different problems; one ladder for all three hides the only useful information.
4. **Character and artifact scope.** With about 300 artifacts, 70 tablets, Miracles, and combos interacting with each branch, a weapon-only ranking measures a fraction of a build.

## Documented "meta" signals, stated carefully

- The **Miracle "Hunter"** had its critical damage cut from 50% to 40% in 0.10.0 — the clearest official signal that a crit-focused setup was overperforming at that time.
- Two Greatsword weapons were **removed** in 0.9.6 "to return later after their weapon effects are improved" — an official admission they were not in a good state.
- The **Blade's** sheathed/unsheathed design and the **Staff's** late arrival (March 2026) make them the least-settled branches; expect their relative strength to move most in future patches.

These are dated facts about balance direction, not tier placements.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there an official Sephiria tier list?

No. TEAM HORAY publishes patch notes, not rankings. Any tier list you find is a community opinion; check whether it states a game version and a test method before trusting it.

### What is the S-tier weapon in Sephiria?

No weapon can be honestly labeled S-tier from the public record. The branches differ by documented role — see the comparison on the [Best Weapon page](/guides/sephiria-best-weapon).

### Do tier lists change with patches?

Yes. Documented examples: the Hunter miracle nerf (0.10.0), Greatsword weapon removals (0.9.6), and new upgrades for Dagger and Greatsword in 1.0.31 (September 2026) all shift relative strength.
