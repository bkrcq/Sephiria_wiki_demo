## Why Switch rumors keep appearing

Switch rumors usually come from one of five sources, and none of them counts as confirmation:

1. **Search suggestions.** A search engine offering "Sephiria Switch" as a suggestion only means other people typed it. Suggestions measure curiosity, not product plans.
2. **Retailer or database placeholders.** A store page or database entry created in advance of any announcement is often a guess, a template, or an error. Until the developer or Nintendo names the product, a placeholder proves nothing.
3. **Handheld confusion.** Sephiria can be played on a handheld PC, which looks like a Switch in a photo or a short video. Playing on handheld PC hardware is not the same as a Nintendo Switch release.
4. **Wishful social posts.** A post that says "I hope this comes to Switch" gets repeated until it reads like news. Check whether the post links to an official source; if it does not, it is not evidence.
5. **Misread interviews or streams.** A developer saying "we would like to" or "we are considering it" is not an announcement. An announcement names the platform, the timing, and the publisher or store destination.

When you see a new Switch claim, ask three questions: who said it, where is the original statement, and does it name a platform, a date, and a store page? If any answer is missing, the claim is unconfirmed.

## What a genuine Switch announcement would look like

A real announcement would be checkable in minutes. It would include most or all of the following:

- A statement from TEAM HORAY, Steam, or Nintendo — not only from a fan account, a rumor account, or an aggregator.
- The platform named explicitly: Nintendo Switch, and whether it also covers any successor hardware.
- Timing: a release date, a release window, or a clear statement that the date is still to be announced.
- A destination: a Nintendo eShop product page, a Nintendo news post, or a Steam news post linking to one.
- Practical details: publisher, price or pricing approach, and whether progress or multiplayer connects with the Steam version.

If a "Switch announcement" has none of these, it is not an announcement. Save yourself the disappointment and wait for the real one.
