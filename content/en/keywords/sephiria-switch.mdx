## Triage a Switch rumor in four passes

1. **Find the speaker.** Identify TEAM HORAY, Nintendo, a retailer, a publication, or a fan account. A verified-looking profile image is not a source chain.
2. **Open the original statement.** Follow quotation links back to the original announcement rather than counting articles that repeat each other. Several copied reports may all depend on one unsupported claim.
3. **Read the exact platform wording.** “Handheld,” “controller,” or “console interest” does not name Nintendo Switch. Keep intentions, development announcements, and released products separate.
4. **Split the claims.** Platform, release window, price, save transfer, and multiplayer compatibility are separate questions. A platform announcement can be real while the date is still to be announced.

Do not require a release date, price, and store listing for an announcement to count: a developer can explicitly announce a platform before providing those details. Missing fields remain open; they do not automatically make the whole announcement false.

## Steam Deck and controller support are different questions

Steam Deck is handheld PC hardware, while Nintendo Switch is a separate platform. A compatibility discussion or controller feature for the Steam product does not identify a Nintendo product. Likewise, an online-co-op feature says how many people may play in the documented product; it says nothing by itself about which other platforms can join.

Do not turn an absent Nintendo announcement into a claim about performance on handheld PCs. This page provides no new Steam Deck compatibility test, performance measurement, or certification.

## Cross-save and cross-play: keep two evidence boxes

**Cross-save** concerns progress transferring between platforms. **Cross-play** concerns multiplayer between platforms. Neither is established for a Steam-to-Switch pairing by the source record here. A future announcement must address each separately; confirmation of one would not confirm the other.

Before planning around either feature, record: the platforms named, the exact feature promised, any stated conditions, and the original announcement link. Leave unanswered fields open. Steam Cloud or four-player co-op alone is not evidence of Nintendo save transfer or cross-platform parties.

## What to look for when an official announcement appears

- An original statement attributable to the developer or Nintendo that explicitly names the platform.
- Release timing, if supplied; distinguish a window from a fixed date.
- A store destination, if supplied, matching the named product and region.
- Price and edition details, if supplied; do not reuse the Steam base price as a Nintendo quote.
- Separate save-transfer and multiplayer statements, if supplied.
- Any later correction, delay, or platform limitation tied to the original announcement.
