## The build layers, as documented by TEAM HORAY

A Sephiria build is not one choice — it is six layers that patch notes and the store page document independently. This page names each layer, what is confirmed about it, and where the record runs out.

*Checked against the official Steam store page and TEAM HORAY's patch notes on October 21, 2026.*

## Layer 1: Weapon branch

Six branches, each with over 50 unique upgrades (200+ total): **Sword and Shield**, **Dagger**, **Greatsword**, **Crossbow**, **Blade**, and **Staff**. Two are unlock-gated: Blade (update 0.8.12) "can be unlocked through fate engraving" — Destiny Inscription — and Staff (update 0.11.0) is "unlocked via Destiny Inscription." The Crossbow is the documented ranged option and uniquely requires reloading; the Blade alternates between sheathed and unsheathed states. Full branch histories are on the [Weapons page](/weapons).

## Layer 2: Artifacts and Tablets in the inventory grid

About **300 artifacts** and **70 tablets** exist at 1.0. The store description states the core build verb: you "collect and arrange Artifacts and empower them with the different Tablets" — placement inside the inventory is a build decision, not storage. Documented sub-systems:

- **Mystic Pot** (0.10.10): insert artifacts to create a Tablet; found in hidden rooms or as combat rewards.
- **Tablet Combiner** (0.10.14): a random object that synthesizes Tablets.
- **Destiny Inscription unlocks** (0.10.14): that update's six artifacts are unlocked through Destiny Inscription.
- **Named artifacts** in patch notes include the Chalcedony Key, Absolute Ring, Bucket of Plump Berries, Rylie's Pocket Watch, Glowing Hourglass, and Academy Fountain Pen — enough to prove individual artifacts have specific, sometimes scaling effects (the Fountain Pen scales Grimoire Damage with Evasion).

## Layer 3: Talents

Talents unlock as you play and persist as long-term progression. Patch notes name two with exact effects: **Willpower** (Wish Fountain capacity and starting dice/HP/MP values by level, rebalanced in 0.10.0) and **Ingenuity** (damage per active combo type, raised from 4% to 7% at level 20 in 0.10.0). The Wish Fountain itself had its base capacity raised from 3 to 4 in the same update.

## Layer 4: Miracles

Miracles are chosen run modifiers. Documented examples: **Hunter** (critical damage adjusted 50% → 40% in 0.10.0) and the three added at 1.0 — **Angler**, **Electromancer**, and **Flame Sword**. Update 1.0.23 fixed connection drops when selecting a Miracle in multiplayer, confirming Miracles are picked in co-op sessions too.

## Layer 5: Item combos and animal synergies

The combo system (overhauled October 2025, refined November 2025) rewards specific item combinations; the **Elemental** combo (0.10.10) is a support combo that enhances your highest elemental attribute. Animal synergies are listed among the game's build combinations on the store page. The **Carrot Soldier** (1.0.31) shows what companions contribute: a summoned ally whose damage is based on your Normal Attack, lasting 20 seconds per summon.

## Layer 6: Costume

Costumes (20+ at 1.0) can carry gameplay effects: patch notes describe starting items obtained from a Costume and at least one costume effect interacting with weapons. Treat your costume as a minor build layer, not a skin.

## Putting it together honestly

No official or reproducibly-tested "best build" exists, so this site will not print one. What the documented record supports is a decision order: pick the branch you execute best → arrange artifacts/tablets around its damage type (the Grimoire stats on the [Grimoire page](/weapons/grimoire) show how deep the interactions go) → let Talents and Miracles cover the bottleneck that ends your runs. The [Build Decision Guide](/guides/sephiria-build-decision-guide) turns that order into a worksheet.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best build in Sephiria?

No verified best build exists. The documented layers above are what a build consists of; strength claims without a game version, a test method, and repeat runs are opinions, not data.

### Do artifacts matter more than the weapon?

The weapon branch defines your attacks (50+ upgrades each); artifacts and tablets modify and empower them through the inventory grid. Patch notes tune both constantly, which is why builds are recorded with a game version on this site.

### What is a Miracle in Sephiria?

A selectable run modifier. Named examples from official notes: Hunter, Angler, Electromancer, and Flame Sword.
