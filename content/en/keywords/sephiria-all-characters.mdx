## Direct answer

TEAM HORAY has not published a complete character roster for Sephiria. What official sources — the Steam store page and the developer's patch notes — do name is a small, specific cast. This page lists exactly those named characters and creatures, with the source context for each, instead of padding the list with guesses.

*Checked against the official Steam store page and patch notes on October 21, 2026.*

## Playable and companion characters

| Character | Role (as documented) | Source context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The bunny protagonist | The player character: a brave bunny who climbs the tower to save Bunnyville from the prophesied demon slaughter on the Day of Destiny | Steam store description |
| Collin | An NPC who can become a companion; update 1.0.24 (Aug 7, 2026) notes "NPC Collin: Dialogue is now disabled after becoming a companion" | Official patch notes, 1.0.24 |
| Carrot Soldier | A summonable companion: "A Companion summoned by the player. Its attack damage is based on the player's Normal Attack damage. Each summon lasts 20 seconds" (via the Sacred Jar weapon effect) | Official patch notes, 1.0.31 |

The Companion system itself is a documented mechanic — patch notes reference companions dying during location transitions (0.11.7) and a "Companion Damage" stat (1.0.31).

The store page promises **over 10 unique bosses** and **more than 60 distinct enemies** in total, so this named list is a floor, not the roster.

## The setting's named places and peoples

Bunnyville is the protagonist's home village, threatened on the "Day of Destiny." Named locations tied to characters in the notes include the Mole Stronghold, the Place of Recollection, and Qliphoth. One artifact — Rylie's Pocket Watch (0.12.0) — carries a personal name (Rylie), but no official source describes who Rylie is, so this page does not promote the name to a character entry.

## What is not documented

No official source publishes character classes, a playable-character select screen, voice cast, or full NPC list. Searches for specific rumored characters (for example "Aiba") return no match in the official store page or patch notes — see the [Aiba page](/guides/aiba-sephiria) for that specific check. When TEAM HORAY names more characters in patch notes, this page is where they will be added, with the update number attached.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is the main character of Sephiria?

An unnamed bunny from Bunnyville, per the official store description. No canonical personal name for the protagonist appears in the official sources checked.

### Can NPCs join you in Sephiria?

Yes — Collin is documented as an NPC who becomes a companion (1.0.24), and the Carrot Soldier is a documented summon (1.0.31). A full companion list has not been published.

### How many characters are in Sephiria?

No official roster count exists. Five bosses and three playable-side characters/summons are named in official sources, against totals of 10+ bosses and 60+ enemies on the store page.
