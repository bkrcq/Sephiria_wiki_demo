## Direct answer

Sephiria has **over 20 costumes**, confirmed by TEAM HORAY's 1.0 launch announcement. The official patch notes also document that costumes are more than skins: appearance customization arrived in June 2025, 7 costumes received additional appearance variants in update 1.0.24, costumes can grant starting items, and at least one costume is unlocked in Chapter 1. A complete costume-by-costume catalogue with names and unlock conditions has **not** been published officially — this page records the documented facts and leaves the catalogue honestly open.

*Checked against the official Steam store page, launch announcement, and patch notes on October 21, 2026.*

## What this tells a player

- **Costumes are a light build layer.** Starting items and a named stat bonus (Skill Damage on the Scholar Lizard Costume) are gameplay effects, not decoration. When you plan a run, your costume choice belongs in the build notes alongside your weapon branch.
- **Unlocks start early.** A Chapter 1 costume unlock is documented, so costume hunting does not begin at endgame.
- **Appearances evolve.** The June 2025 appearance system plus the 1.0.24 variants mean a costume's look can change independent of its effects; patch notes treat appearance options as a live feature.

## What remains undocumented

No official list names all 20+ costumes, their unlock conditions, or their full effects. This page will not invent names to fill the table. If you document a costume in-game, the fields that make the observation useful are: name exactly as shown, how it was obtained (chapter/shop/event), its stated effect, any starting item granted, and the game version — the same evidence standard used across this wiki.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many costumes are in Sephiria?

Over 20, per the official 1.0 launch announcement.

### Do costumes affect gameplay in Sephiria?

Yes, in documented ways: at least one costume grants starting items, and the Scholar Lizard Costume is documented as increasing Skill Damage. Costumes are not purely cosmetic.

### How do you unlock costumes in Sephiria?

At least one costume is unlocked in Chapter 1 (fixed in 1.0.31). A complete official unlock list has not been published, so specific per-costume conditions remain undocumented.

### What is the Scholar Lizard Costume?

A costume named in update 1.0.33, where its Skill Damage increase was fixed so it "now correctly applies." It is one of the few costumes named in official notes.
