## What Sephiria is

Sephiria is a top-down action roguelite by TEAM HORAY, the studio behind Dungreed. On the Day of Destiny, a terrible omen strikes Bunnyville: the bunny villagers will be slaughtered by a horde of demons unless you stop them. You play a brave bunny climbing a tower through **6 chapters**, fighting **more than 60 distinct enemies** and **over 10 unique bosses**, to uncover the threat at the journey's end. The game left Early Access with version 1.0 on **July 31, 2026** for Windows and macOS on Steam.

*Facts on this page are drawn from the official Steam store page and TEAM HORAY's patch notes, checked October 21, 2026.*

## The systems you will actually use

| System | Confirmed facts |
| --- | --- |
| Weapons | 6 branches — Sword and Shield, Dagger, Greatsword, Crossbow, Blade, Staff — each with over 50 unique upgrades (200+ total). Blade and Staff are unlocked through Destiny Inscription. |
| Artifacts & Tablets | About 300 artifacts and about 70 tablets. You collect and **arrange** them in your inventory grid; tablets empower the artifacts they are placed with. |
| Talents | New Talents unlock each run; named examples in patch notes include Willpower and Ingenuity, which modify things like Wish Fountain capacity and combo damage. |
| Miracles | A selectable run modifier system. The Hunter miracle's critical damage was adjusted from 50% to 40% in update 0.10.0; Angler, Electromancer, and Flame Sword were added at 1.0. |
| Item combos | Combinations of items grant combo effects; the Elemental combo enhances your highest elemental attribute. The combo system was overhauled in October 2025. |
| Co-op | Online co-op for up to 4 players: trade items, revive fallen companions, explore the tower together. |
| Training Grounds | Added in update 0.11.5 (April 2026); a Comprehensive Training Grounds with a tutorial followed in update 1.0.31. |

## A beginner's first hour, from documented mechanics

1. **Start with Sword and Shield.** It is the branch documented in the earliest updates, and its guard is the most forgiving tool while you learn enemy patterns. Patch notes show the developer still tunes it (a new upgrade path arrived in 0.11.0).
2. **Treat the inventory as part of combat.** The store description calls the inventory system "strategic": artifacts gain power from the tablets arranged with them, so where you place things matters, not just what you pick up.
3. **Unlock a Talent every run.** Talents persist as your long-term progression lever; early patch-note examples (Willpower raising Wish Fountain capacity) show they change how a run feels, not just its numbers.
4. **Use the Training Grounds.** They exist specifically so you can test a weapon branch before committing a run to it.
5. **Expect the Destiny Inscription to matter.** Two of the six weapon branches (Blade, Staff) and batches of artifacts are unlocked through it — the tree at the end of Chapter 5 is where the Chapter 5 notes place it.

## Controls and requirements (official Steam listing)

- **Full controller support**, including DualShock and DualSense controllers, is listed on the Steam store page, alongside Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud.
- Minimum PC requirements from the store page: Windows 10 64-bit, Ryzen 5 1400 or better, 8 GB RAM, GeForce GTX 750 or better, DirectX 11, and 2 GB of storage.
- The game supports 15 languages, including English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Russian.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Sephiria good for roguelite beginners?

Its documented structure is beginner-friendly: a Training Grounds for practice, Talents that accumulate between runs, and a first branch (Sword and Shield) built around guarding. The full release is rated "Very Positive" on Steam (about 95% of 13,485 reviews, checked October 21, 2026).

### How many players is Sephiria?

Single-player, plus online co-op for up to 4 players with item trading and revives, per the Steam store description.

### What should I unlock first?

The unlocks with the widest documented impact are the Destiny Inscription branches (Blade and Staff) and run Talents. There is no official "unlock first" ranking; prioritize the branch you can execute consistently.
