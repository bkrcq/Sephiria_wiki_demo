## Direct answer

**Sephiria has 6 chapters.** That count comes from TEAM HORAY's own materials: the Steam store description describes "a journey spanning 6 chapters," and the 1.0 launch announcement (July 31, 2026) lists 6 chapters as the full-release structure, with Chapter 6 and the game's ending added in that update.

The chapters were not all there at Early Access launch. They arrived one at a time in major updates, which is why older sources disagree about the count.

*Chapter record below verified against TEAM HORAY's official Steam patch notes on October 21, 2026.*

## Bosses per chapter — what is documented

The Steam store description promises **over 10 unique bosses** across the journey, plus more than 60 distinct enemies. The patch notes name individual additions rather than publishing a full boss roster:

- Chapter 3 added 1 new boss (0.8.3).
- Chapter 4 added 2 new bosses (0.10.0).
- Chapter 5 added 1 new boss and 1 new mid-boss (0.11.0).
- Chapter 6 added 2 new bosses and 1 new mini-boss (1.0).

Bosses named in official notes include Kraz, the Guardian Demon of Qliphoth; Odner, the Blasting Expert; Mogdi, the Black Hand Mole; Haz, the Mind-Eater Root; and the Demonic Grimoire miniboss. A complete official boss-by-chapter table has not been published, so this page does not assign every named boss to a chapter beyond what the notes state.

## How long is each chapter?

TEAM HORAY has not published official chapter lengths or a total clear-time figure, and this page will not invent one. What the structure does tell you: the game is a run-based roguelite, so a "chapter" is progressed through repeated runs rather than walked once, and later chapters assume builds assembled from the roughly 300 artifacts, 70 tablets, and 200+ weapon upgrades the game documents. Your time per chapter will depend mostly on how many runs your build needs — not on a fixed hour count anyone can quote honestly.

## Named places you will hear about

Patch notes name several locations tied to chapter progression: the Rooted Cave, the Library (home of a boss added in July 2025), the Chapter 4 Woodlands, the Mole Stronghold and the Place of Recollection (both referenced in the Chapter 5 notes), and Qliphoth in Chapter 5. These are the documented landmarks; a full room-by-room route is more than the official sources support.

## Hard Mode is separate from the chapter count

Hard Mode is not extra chapters. The 1.0 launch announcement lists **18 Hard Mode elements** as a separate feature line next to the 6 chapters, and Hard Mode received its own expansions during Early Access (including a June 2025 expansion and a "Boss Enhancement" mode previewed in May 2025). Finishing Chapter 6 completes the story; Hard Mode is the post-structure challenge layer.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many chapters are in Sephiria?

Six. The count is stated in the Steam store description and in TEAM HORAY's 1.0 launch announcement of July 31, 2026.

### When was the final chapter added?

Chapter 6 arrived with version 1.0 on July 31, 2026, together with the game's ending. Before that date, the game had five chapters.

### Does Hard Mode add more chapters?

No. Hard Mode is a separate challenge structure (18 elements at 1.0) layered on the same six chapters.

### Are the chapter names confirmed?

Official patch notes refer to chapters by number (Chapter 3, Chapter 4, and so on) rather than by unique titles, so this page lists them by number with their update dates and documented contents.
