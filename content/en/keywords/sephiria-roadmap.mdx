## What the pattern tells you

- **Cadence:** major content roughly every 2–3 months during Early Access (a chapter or a weapon each time), shifting to fast, smaller patches after 1.0.
- **Announced-ahead items actually shipped:** the May 2025 "Future Updates Preview" promised the Crossbow, Chapter 3, Chapter 4, a katana-style weapon (which shipped as the Blade), costume layering, and multiplayer QoL — all of which appear in the later record. This developer's previews have a good delivery rate, which is the honest way to read any future preview post.
- **Not announced:** no post-1.0 content roadmap, no console versions, and no DLC have been announced as of October 21, 2026. Treat any claim otherwise as unconfirmed.
