## Price context a review should include

Sephiria launched at **$14.99** and, as TEAM HORAY announced in advance, the price rose to **$17.99 on September 14, 2026** (regional examples from the notice: ₩16,500 → ₩19,000; ¥1,700 → ¥1,980; ¥58 → ¥68; €14.79 → €18.25). Reviews written before and after that date are evaluating two different prices; the game did not get more expensive quietly.

## What the score is reviewing

The "Very Positive" consensus is about a finished game, not the Early Access build: version 1.0 (July 31, 2026) shipped 6 chapters, an ending, 6 weapon branches with 200+ upgrades, about 300 artifacts, and 4-player online co-op — after **484 days of Early Access and 58 updates**, per the launch announcement. Post-launch support has continued at the same cadence (patches through 1.0.33 on September 11, 2026, plus a Tokyo Game Show 2026 appearance), which is the pattern review-watchers usually want to see.

## What this page is not

This is a reception record, not a scored review by this site. Sephiria Wiki does not publish a numeric rating of its own, and it does not quote individual player reviews it cannot attribute. For hands-on impressions, the Steam review section itself — filterable by playtime and date — is the primary source.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Sephiria worth it?

The verifiable signals: a "Very Positive" Steam consensus (about 95% of 13,485 reviews, October 21, 2026), a finished 1.0 with an ending, and active post-launch patching. At $17.99, whether that is worth it depends on your taste for run-based roguelites — the content totals (6 chapters, 60+ enemies, 10+ bosses) are documented on the [beginner guide](/guides/sephiria-guide).

### Did Sephiria's reviews improve after 1.0?

The all-time positive share (about 95%) sits above the 93% recent-review figure recorded in earlier research, but the two snapshots measure different windows; this page does not claim a trend from two non-matching samples.

### Is Sephiria finished or still in Early Access?

Finished. Version 1.0 launched July 31, 2026 with the ending added; the game is no longer in Early Access.
