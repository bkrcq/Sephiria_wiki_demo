# Sephiria Home Research Notes

*Last updated: October 14, 2026.*

The home page uses verified research: 6 chapters, 300 artifacts, 70 tablets, 6 weapon branches, 200+ weapon upgrades, 60-level Hard Mode, and online co-op for up to four players.

No official redemption-code system has been announced. The code panel therefore displays **暂无**.

## How to use this wiki

Start with your run goal, then pick the guide that matches it. New players should begin with the [beginner guide](/guides/sephiria-guide). Players choosing between builds should use the [build decision guide](/guides/sephiria-build-decision-guide), a step-by-step worksheet for diagnosing the bottleneck that actually ends their runs. Players planning upgrades should read the [weapons page](/weapons) and the [upgrade tree page](/guides/sephiria-upgrade-tree) together: the first explains how to choose and test a weapon branch, the second explains how to plan upgrade routes without an invented node-by-node map.

## What this wiki will and will not claim

This wiki separates source-backed facts from details that remain unconfirmed. Counts such as 300 artifacts and 200+ upgrades describe the size of the game's systems; they do not identify individual entries, effects, or rankings. When a detail cannot be traced to an official source or reproducible in-game evidence, the page says so instead of filling the gap with a guess. See the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) for the source hierarchy and correction method.
